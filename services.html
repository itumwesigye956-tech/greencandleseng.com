Our Services

Professional engineering solutions designed for reliability, efficiency and sustainability

At Green Candles Engineering, we deliver end-to-end engineering solutions across electrical systems, renewable energy, industrial automation, and construction. Our team combines technical expertise with practical experience to ensure every project meets the highest standards of quality and safety.

Electrical Engineering

Complete design, installation, testing and commissioning of electrical systems for commercial, industrial and institutional facilities. We handle power distribution, lighting, earthing and protection systems.

Renewable Energy Solutions

Design and installation of solar PV systems, hybrid power solutions and energy storage. We help clients reduce energy costs while supporting sustainable development goals.

Industrial Systems & Automation

Supply and integration of industrial control systems, motor control centres, variable frequency drives (VFDs), PLCs and process automation for manufacturing plants.

Construction & Project Delivery

Full project management and execution of electrical and mechanical works. From design to final handover, we ensure quality, safety and timely delivery.

Manufacturing Support

Custom design and fabrication of control panels, switchboards and electrical assemblies. We also provide equipment installation and commissioning services.

Maintenance & Technical Support

Preventive and corrective maintenance services, system upgrades, troubleshooting and long-term technical support to keep your facilities running efficiently.
